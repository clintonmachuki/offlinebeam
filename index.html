i
A different kind of wireless.

QR transfer is deliberately simple, but it's slower than Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth. Small files are best; large videos can take a long time. The app must be opened twice while online so its scanner can be cached. Camera scanning requires HTTPS and browser camera permission. QR frames are not encrypted in this prototype, so only transfer files you are comfortable showing on both screens.
